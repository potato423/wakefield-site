FLOWPOD / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I use my own model now?

Not yet on the public site. The car, ship and plane scenes are interactive samples. We are inviting early testers for personal model experiments.

Which files are you interested in?

Tell us if you have an STL, OBJ or GLB model in your beta request. We will check each early model before promising a result.

Is this an engineering wind tunnel?

No. The public preview is a 2D visual flow simulation intended to make models come alive. It does not provide validated drag or lift measurements.

What will it cost?

Joining the beta is free. The prices on the homepage are proposals for future personal renders. No payment is taken today.

What happens after I join?

We receive your answers by email and may contact you about a beta place or ask more about your model. A request does not guarantee an invitation.
