Privacy.

Last updated October 21, 2026.

What we collect

If you join the beta, you provide your email address, model description, preferred flow and answer to what else you would like Flowpod to make possible. The public preview does not upload your 3D model.

How your request reaches us

The form sends your answers through FormSubmit to the Flowpod owner's inbox. If the online form fails and you choose the email fallback, your email app sends the same answers instead. We use your answers to review beta interest and contact you about Flowpod. We do not sell your answers.

Site services and cookies

Cloudflare hosts this site and may process technical request data needed to serve it. Google Fonts supplies the page fonts and may receive your browser's request for those files. Flowpod does not set analytics or advertising cookies on this page.

Deletion and contact

To ask us to remove your beta request or ask how it is used, email jej03701@gmail.com. Messages and replies are kept only as needed to run the beta and handle your request.
